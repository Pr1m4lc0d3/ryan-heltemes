The Aletheia Papers

Research into how symbolically dense language shapes a model's interpretive posture before it reasons — and how an explicit, opt-in constraint can steady what comes out without controlling it. The work is model-agnostic, and it does not prescribe outcomes, beliefs or conclusions.

Everything below is free and downloads straight from this site. No signup, no email, nothing collected.
