The Garden of Revolution

A seven-book cycle, and not sequels in the ordinary sense — the author calls them living phases of one transmission. It starts by naming what has happened to shared language and shared story, and then turns, volume by volume, from diagnosis to construction: what a person can actually build that cannot be turned against them.
